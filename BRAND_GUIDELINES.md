# Aeoth Agent Brand Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

Aeoth Agent is the product name used by this repository. Use it to identify this project and its public interfaces without implying endorsement, sponsorship, or ownership by an external organization.

- Describe the project accurately: "built with Aeoth Agent" or "compatible with Aeoth Agent" is appropriate when it is true.
- Do not claim that a plugin, integration, fork, or distribution is official unless the repository explicitly says so.
- Do not reuse third-party logos, screenshots, domains, or community channels in a way that suggests affiliation or authorization.
- Preserve the copyright, license, source, and attribution notices of upstream and third-party components.
- Keep compatibility identifiers such as package names, protocol fields, and environment variables unchanged when changing them would break an existing contract; explain that boundary instead of presenting those identifiers as the product brand.

These guidelines describe this repository's naming and attribution expectations. They do not grant trademark rights or permission to use any third party's marks.
